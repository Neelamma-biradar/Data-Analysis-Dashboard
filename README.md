# Adidas Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

The **Adidas Sales Analysis** project is a data analytics and business intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI**. The main objective of this project is to analyze Adidas sales data and understand overall sales performance, profitability, product performance, retailer contribution, regional performance, and sales trends over time.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, transformation, analysis, KPI calculation, and interactive visualization. The final Power BI dashboard provides an easy-to-understand view of the company's sales performance and helps identify important trends and patterns in the data.

## Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to answer important business questions such as:

* What is the total sales and operating profit?
* Which products generate the highest sales?
* Which products contribute the most to profit?
* Which regions generate the highest revenue?
* Which retailers have the best sales performance?
* How do sales change over different months and years?
* Which products or regions have lower sales performance?
* What are the major sales trends and patterns?
* How are units sold distributed across different products and regions?

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains Adidas sales-related information, including:

* **Retailer** – Name of the retailer selling Adidas products.
* **Region** – Geographic region of the sale.
* **State and City** – Location-level sales information.
* **Product** – Adidas product information.
* **Sales Date** – Date on which the transaction occurred.
* **Units Sold** – Number of products sold.
* **Price per Unit** – Selling price of the product.
* **Total Sales** – Revenue generated from the transaction.
* **Operating Profit** – Profit generated from sales.
* **Operating Margin** – Profitability percentage.
* **Sales Method** – Method or channel through which the product was sold.

## Data Cleaning and Transformation

Before creating the dashboard, the raw data was reviewed and prepared for analysis. **Power Query** was used to perform data cleaning and transformation tasks such as checking missing values, removing duplicate records, correcting data types, formatting date fields, and preparing columns required for analysis.

The cleaned dataset was then loaded into Power BI for further analysis and visualization. Appropriate relationships and data structures were prepared to make the dashboard efficient and easy to use.

## Data Analysis

The project focuses on analyzing Adidas sales performance from multiple perspectives.

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis

The dashboard analyzes total sales, total units sold, and operating profit to provide an overview of the overall business performance. KPI cards are used to display important metrics clearly.

### 2. Product Performance Analysis

Different Adidas products are compared based on sales, units sold, and profit. This helps identify high-performing products and understand which products contribute significantly to overall revenue.

### 3. Regional Analysis

Sales performance is analyzed across different regions, states, and cities. This helps identify locations with higher sales contributions and understand geographic sales patterns.

### 4. Retailer Analysis

The project compares different retailers based on sales and profit. This helps understand which retailers contribute significantly to Adidas's overall sales performance.

### 5. Time-Based Analysis

Monthly and yearly sales trends are analyzed to understand how sales change over time. Line charts and other time-based visualizations help identify increases, decreases, and seasonal patterns in sales.

### 6. Profitability Analysis

Operating profit and operating margin are analyzed to understand the profitability of products, regions, and retailers. This helps identify areas that generate higher revenue as well as stronger profit contribution.

## Power BI Dashboard

The final dashboard contains interactive visualizations and KPI cards that provide a complete overview of Adidas sales performance.

The dashboard includes:

* Total Sales KPI
* Total Units Sold KPI
* Total Operating Profit KPI
* Operating Margin KPI
* Sales by Product
* Sales by Region
* Sales by Retailer
* Monthly Sales Trends
* Profit Analysis
* Product Performance
* Geographic Sales Analysis
* Interactive slicers and filters

Users can interact with the dashboard by selecting different **products, retailers, regions, and time periods** to analyze specific areas of the business.
